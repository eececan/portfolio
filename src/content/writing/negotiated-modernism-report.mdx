## Method

{/* One subsection per point. The pipeline stages in notebook order:
    1. Climate lookup from coordinates
    2. Glazing ratio from hand-marked window regions
    3. Pilotis via Hough line detection in a fractional facade band
    4. Open plan via adaptive flood fill → area / density / convexity
    5. Free facade via drawn glass extent ratio
    6. Flat roof via CLIP zero-shot + geometric slope veto */}

### Free facade

| Building | Span (px) | Glass (px) | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| Villa Savoye | 807 | 807 | 1.00 |
| Casa das Canoas | 508 | 497 | 0.98 |
| Frey House II | 724 | 704 | 0.97 |
| Kaufmann Desert House | 1200 | 997 | 0.83 |

All four pass. Contrast with glazing: the free facade as a *structural* idea transfers
intact, while the ribbon window as a *proportion* does not.

### Flat roof classification

| Building | Flat roof | p(flat) | Slope index | Veto |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Villa Savoye | yes | 0.962 | 0.009 | — |
| Casa das Canoas | yes | 0.695 | 0.075 | — |
| Kaufmann Desert House | yes | 0.860 | 0.150 | — |
| Frey House II | no | 0.486 | 0.967 | mono-pitch |
| Control (pitched) | no | 0.256 | 0.000 | — |

Accuracy 5/5 — but Frey House II is decided on a CLIP margin of 0.03, rescued by the
geometric veto. Say so plainly; it is a correct answer for the wrong reason.
